Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take? +

Most website projects are completed within 2–4 weeks. E-commerce stores may take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity. Automation projects typically take 1–3 weeks.

Do you work with international clients? +

Absolutely. We work with clients across India, the UK, US, and beyond. All communication and deliverables are in English, and we accommodate different time zones for meetings.

What do you need from me to get started? +

A brief description of your business, your goals, and any reference sites you admire. We handle the rest — you don't need to be technical at all.

Is post-launch support included? +

Yes — all projects include 30 days of post-launch support at no extra charge. Ongoing maintenance and support packages are available after that.
