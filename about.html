Who We Are

A Studio That Believes in the Craft

LibertyStack Solutions was born from a simple conviction: every business — whether a local boutique or a global brand — deserves a digital presence that truly reflects its quality.

We are a lean, focused studio that brings big-agency thinking to every project, without the overhead, delays, or impersonal treatment. When you work with us, you work directly with the person building your product.

Our work spans website design, e-commerce development, brand redesigns, and intelligent AI automation — all united by a commitment to craft, clarity, and measurable results.

The Founder

Meet Ishant

Ishant is a self-taught developer, designer, and digital strategist with a genuine passion for building things that work beautifully. Based in Patna, Bihar, he has spent years honing his craft across website development, UI/UX design, and modern automation.

What sets Ishant apart is his rare ability to see the whole picture — from the visual identity of a brand to the underlying systems that power it. Every project he takes on receives his full attention, from initial discovery through to post-launch support.

His approach is simple: listen deeply, think clearly, and build something you'll be proud to show the world.
